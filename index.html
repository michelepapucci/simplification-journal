Simplification data explorer

READ-IT distributions and linguistic profiles of the Wikipedia original–simplified pairs used to train and test the readability-controlled simplification models. The analyses are those of Papucci, Venturi & Dell’Orletta (CLiC-it 2025), re-run on this data and broken down by strategy and control token.

The data at a glance

Pairs per control token

Each training pair appears once per strategy, with that strategy’s control token. The training set has 57,720 pairs; within each slice the analyses keep one simplification per original sentence (the smaller “used” figure). Click a cell to inspect that slice below.
