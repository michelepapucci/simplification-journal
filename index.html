Simplification data explorer

READ-IT distributions and linguistic profiles of the original–simplified pairs used to train and test the readability-controlled simplification models, taken from the Wikipedia part of IMPaCTS. The analyses are those of Papucci, Venturi & Dell’Orletta (CLiC-it 2025), re-run on each dataset and broken down by control token.

Datasets compared

The same analyses on every dataset, one simplification per original. Drops are mean READ-IT of the originals minus mean READ-IT of the simplifications (0–100). Pillai’s trace is from the two-group MANOVA (Table 4 of the 2025 paper) on the randomly selected, most and least simplified version of each original. The feature columns give the rank-biserial r: above 0, the feature is higher in the original. Click a row to switch dataset.

Linguistic profile

Rank-biserial r of the Wilcoxon signed-rank test, original vs simplified, for each control token. Features are ordered by mean |r| across the columns; blank cells are not significant (p ≥ 0.05). The bars above give Pillai’s trace of the MANOVA for each column, with a tick at the value expected by chance. Click a cell to select that control token.
